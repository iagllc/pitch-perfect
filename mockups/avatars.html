5. How it would work in the game

  • You pick once. Boy/Girl, skin, hair, color, extra. Saved on the device. Changeable from the menu (a little "Edit avatar" button next to your name).
  • Teammates and opponents get random avatars with their jersey names, so the field looks like a real mixed team. Keepers get gloves.
  • Online: your avatar travels with your name, so friends see your look on their screens.
  • Where it shows: the match, First Game, Online, and every drill. Explainer mini-maps keep simple dots (clearer at that size).
  • Faces the way you run, legs pump while moving, a quick kick pose on pass/shoot, arms-up jump on a goal.
  • Performance: about 12 shapes per player. Same cost as the current circles plus text. No images to load.
